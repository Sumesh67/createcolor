Pinterest is a search engine — these pins keep ranking for months. Pin consistently (3–5/day) and each links back to a theme page that funnels to your app.

**Pin images:** ready-made **vertical 1000×1500** pins (title banner + art + CTA footer) live in `/public/gallery/pins/` — also downloadable at `https://createandcolor.aivantageworks.com/gallery/pins/<file>`.

## One-time setup (15 min)
1. Create a **free Pinterest Business account** (pinterest.com/business/create) → unlocks analytics + Rich Pins.
2. **Claim your website** (Settings → Claimed accounts) so every pin credits you and gets analytics.
3. Create these **boards** (name them exactly — board names are searchable):
   - Dinosaur Coloring Pages
   - Unicorn Coloring Pages
   - Space Coloring Pages
   - Animal Coloring Pages
   - Printable Coloring Pages for Kids  ← general catch-all board
   - Kids Birthday Party Activities
   - Homeschool & Classroom Printables
   - Rainy Day Activities for Kids
4. Pin each card below to its matching theme board **and** the general "Printable Coloring Pages for Kids" board (spacing repins a few days apart).

## Posting cadence (free, sustainable)
- **3–5 pins/day**, spaced out. Use Pinterest's native scheduler (free) to batch a week at once.
- Pin the **same image to multiple relevant boards** a few days apart — totally fine on Pinterest.
- **Seasonal push:** 6–8 weeks before Halloween/Christmas, pin the Holiday card heavily and make a dedicated "Halloween Coloring Pages" / "Christmas Coloring Pages" board.
- Re-pin your best performers (check Pinterest Analytics monthly) — winners compound.

## Make new pins for free, anytime
Every theme page can produce endless fresh pin images: open your app, generate a few pages on that theme, screenshot, and pin. More unique pins → more search surface area.
