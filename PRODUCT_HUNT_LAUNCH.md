## First comment (the maker's story — post immediately after launch)

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
>
> I built CreateAndColor because my kid never wanted to color what was in the coloring books — it was always something oddly specific like "a shark riding a unicorn" or "a monster truck birthday cake." I'd spend ages searching for printables that didn't exist.
>
> So I made an app that just... makes them. You type it, say it out loud (great for kids who can't read yet), or spin a slot machine for a surprise — and you get a printable black-and-white coloring page in a few seconds.
>
> A few things people seem to love:
> 🎨 It's never the same page twice — endless and unique
> 🎤 Voice input so little kids can create on their own
> 🎉 Party Pack: 20 custom themed pages at once for birthdays/classrooms
> 📸 Magic Lens: turn a photo of a toy or pet into a coloring page
> 🖨️ Print at home, totally screen-free once it's on paper
>
> It's free to try on iOS and the web. I'd genuinely love feedback from parents, teachers, and anyone who colors — what themes should I add next?
>
> Thanks for checking it out! 🙏

## Launch-day playbook
- **When:** Post at **12:01 AM PT** (PH day resets then) — a full day = more time to gather upvotes. Tue–Thu tend to be strongest.
- **First hour is critical:** message friends/family/existing users to check it out (don't say "upvote" — PH filters that; say "I launched, would love your feedback").
- **Reply to every single comment** fast — engagement boosts ranking.
- Add the **Product Hunt "Featured on" badge** to your site footer afterward (extra backlink + trust).
- Cross-post the launch to your Reddit/FB/Pinterest audiences and any email list.

## Reply templates for common questions
- **"How is this different from [X]?"** → "Most tools are a fixed library of pages. CreateAndColor generates a brand-new page for literally any idea, so kids never run out and can color exactly what they're imagining."
- **"Is it really free?"** → "Yes — you can create and print pages for free. There's a Party Pack and teacher mode for heavier use."
- **"What ages?"** → "Roughly 2–10. Voice input makes it work even for kids who can't read/type yet."
- **"Android?"** → "It runs great in any mobile browser today at createandcolor.aivantageworks.com — native Android is on the roadmap." *(adjust to your truth)*
